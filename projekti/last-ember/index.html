Why we open after midnight

We think the best flavours come out once the rush is over. The meat rests, the fire breathes, and every burger is made for you, not for a queue.

We started in 2021 with one grill and the idea that the city deserves a real meal at two in the morning. Today the beef arrives fresh every morning, the buns are baked in our own bakery, and the sauces are still mixed by hand every evening before we open.

We named the place after the last ember: what is left when the flames settle. It makes no noise, but it stays warm the longest.
